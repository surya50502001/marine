About Classic Marine

Your Trusted Maritime Partner for Ship Supplies & Precision Workshop Solutions

Established in 2013 and headquartered in Sharjah, U.A.E., Classic Marine has grown to become a cornerstone of the Middle East maritime and industrial ecosystem. Our dual-focus model empowers commercial fleet operators, ship managers, offshore drilling contractors, and industrial plants to source certified deck/engine supplies and commission turnkey metal fabrication from one accountable partner.

Our modern workshop situated in the Sajja Industrial Area (Sharjah) is fully equipped with advanced CNC machinery, heavy shearing, plate rolling, 400T bending, precision lathes, and certified welding bays. Alongside our trading division, we maintain a robust inventory ready for immediate boarding and delivery to all UAE and GCC berths.

Marine Engineering & Workshop Services

Equipped with state-of-the-art metalworking machinery, 15kW CNC fiber laser, cold waterjet cutting, 400T bending, precision lathes, and ASME/DNV class-coded welders for all marine & industrial fabrication.

Heavy & Light Metal Fabrication

Custom manufacturing of marine structural skids, deck gangways, storage tanks, mooring brackets, bollards, equipment foundations, and vessel access platforms.

Material: SS316L, Carbon Steel, Aluminum Class 6G

15kW CNC Laser & Waterjet Cutting

High-precision 2D/3D profile cutting with ultra-tight tolerances. Waterjet cold cutting eliminates heat-affected zones on specialized marine alloys, brass, and composite gaskets.

Thickness: Up to 35mm Steel / 25mm SS ±0.05mm

400T Press Brake & Plate Rolling

Heavy-duty hydraulic CNC bending for complex hull plates, channel profiles, vessel stiffeners, cone rolling, and cylinder rolling for marine pressure vessels.

Bed Length: 4,000mm CNC Hydraulic 400-Ton

Precision Lathe & Milling Machining

Turning, boring, milling, and keyway slotting for propeller shafts, rudder pintles, intermediate shaft bearings, stern tube bushes, and custom flange adapters.

Lathe Capacity: Ø800mm x 4,500mm ISO Gr. 6

High-Pressure Marine Piping & Spools

Prefabricated ballast lines, sea chest manifolds, fire main lines, hydraulic high-pressure tubing, and scrubber overboard lines compliant with class rules.

Hydrotest: Up to 350 Bar Pressure ASME B31.3

Afloat Repairs & On-Board Attendance

Mobile certified flying squads equipped for hot work, hull insert renewals, boiler retubing, pump overhaul, hatch cover repairs, and emergency anchoring gear rectification.
