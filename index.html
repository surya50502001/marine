ABOUT AEGIS

Your Reliable Trading & Sourcing Partner

Aegis International Trading FZ LLC is a UAE-based general trading company specializing in building materials, hardware, tools, safety products, electrical materials, measurement instruments and other essential supplies for construction, maintenance, industrial and commercial requirements.

With a strong sourcing network across the UAE, China, India and other international markets, we work to identify the right products for our customers at competitive prices.

Whether you need a standard product or a specific item that is difficult to source, our team works with trusted manufacturers, suppliers and distributors to identify suitable options.
