# Aegis International Trading FZ LLC (AITFL)

A modern, high-precision commercial marine and offshore engineering platform built for **Aegis International Trading FZ LLC** (UAE & Oman), featuring an Apple-inspired minimalist design system.

---

## ⚓ Core Business Divisions

- **Marine Rigging & Lifting (Roma Standards)**: Galvanized Short Link (DIN 766), Grade 80 & 100 Alloy Lifting Chains (EN 818-2), 6x36 WS IWRC Wire Ropes, Bow Shackles (RR-C-271D).
- **CNC Metal Fabrication (Safari Metal Standards)**: 15kW CNC Fiber Laser Cutting (up to 30mm MS / 25mm SS316L), 400-Ton Press Brake Bending, Custom Offshore Skids (DNV 2.7-1).
- **Shipyard & Afloat Repairs (MFE Oman Standards)**: 24/7 Voyage Engineering, 6G/6GR Class Coded Hull Plate Welding, ASME B31.3 Pipe Spooling, Deck Machinery Overhauls.
- **Marine Technical Chemicals (Oasis Chemical Standards)**: HD-90 Solvent Engine Degreasers, SafeScale-Eco Inhibited Titanium Plate Cooler Descalers, IMO/ROPME Oil Spill Dispersants.

---

##  UI & UX Design System (Apple Aesthetic)

- **Cupertino Design Language**: Deep contrast dark surfaces (`#000000`, `#161617`), frosted glass navigation (`backdrop-blur-xl`), and SF Pro / Inter typography.
- **Bento Grid Architecture**: High-legibility bento boxes with generous whitespace and 1px borders.
- **Segmented iOS-Style Controls**: Clean pill selectors for product category browsing and instant mathematical calculators (WLL, CIP Descaling, Sheet Mass).
- **Slide-Over Sheet Requisition**: Integrated RFQ proforma generator for shipowners and procurement managers across GCC ports.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

Open `index.html` directly in any web browser, or launch the local server with `start.bat`.

python -m http.server 8000
